## Understanding Trim Lines, Safe Zones, and Bleed Margins

* **Bleed Line (3.75 x 2.25 in):** The background color must extend to this edge so cutting variations don't leave white borders.
* **Trim Line (3.50 x 2.00 in):** The exact mechanical cutting line.
* **Safety Margin (3.25 x 1.75 in):** Keep all critical text and logos inside this zone to ensure nothing is clipped.
