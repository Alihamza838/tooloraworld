## Mid-Market Rates vs Retail Bank Spreads Explained

When converting foreign money, you encounter three rate tiers:

1. **Interbank (Mid-Market) Rate:** The wholesale price financial institutions trade amongst themselves. This is the rate Toolora displays.
2. **Retail Credit Card Rate:** The Visa/Mastercard reference rate plus a typical 1% - 3% foreign transaction fee.
3. **Airport Kiosk / Tourist Bureau Rate:** Often marked up by 8% to 15% with additional 'commission' surcharges.

## The 'Dynamic Currency Conversion' (DCC) Scam at Foreign ATMs

When paying at a restaurant in Paris or withdrawing cash in Tokyo, the terminal asks: *'Would you like to be charged in your home currency (USD) or local currency (EUR/JPY)?'* Always select **LOCAL CURRENCY**. Selecting your home currency allows the merchant's terminal to apply a predatory 5% to 8% conversion fee.
