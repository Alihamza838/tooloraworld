# How to Extract Chapters, Custom Page Ranges & Split Massive PDFs Without Quality Loss

*Published on August 16, 2026 | 11 min read | Author: Hamza Tariq*

## Summary
Learn how to extract individual pages, split multi-gigabyte document packets into chapter files, burst single sheets, and isolate confidential sections entirely within your browser.

## Understanding the Three Primary PDF Splitting Methodologies

Depending on your organizational goal, different splitting approaches apply:

1. **Selective Range Extraction:** Isolates specific sections (e.g., pulling a 4-page Non-Disclosure Agreement from a 150-page vendor contract).
2. **Page-by-Page Bursting:** Breaks every single page into an autonomous file (ideal for batch scanning invoices that must be filed by invoice number).
3. **Fixed Interval Division:** Slices a large textbook or training manual into equal 20-page modules for student distribution.
